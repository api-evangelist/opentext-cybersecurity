# OpenText Cybersecurity

> OpenText Cybersecurity (cybersecurity.opentext.com) delivers cybersecurity without the complexity: endpoint protection, email security, and data resilience built for growing businesses and the managed service providers (MSPs) who support them. Backed by 25+ years of experience, 150K+ customers, and 15K+ partners, our integrated solutions are designed to be easy to deploy and to scale with the business.

This site provides authoritative, high-level information about OpenText Cybersecurity products, platforms, and solutions. It is intended for security decision-makers, IT teams, and managed service providers evaluating enterprise and SMB cybersecurity. The scope is product and solution overviews, core capabilities, use cases, and deployment context; marketing CTAs, partner recruitment, and pricing flows are secondary.

Primary audience: Managed Service Providers (MSPs); CISOs and security leaders; IT and security operations teams; and SMB IT administrators.

## Products

- [Endpoint Security](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/endpoint-security/): AI-powered threat detection and prevention across devices.
  - [Endpoint Protection](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/endpoint-security/endpoint-protection/): Multi-vector malware and ransomware prevention for endpoints.
  - [Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR)](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/endpoint-security/endpoint-detection-response/): Detection, investigation, and response for endpoint threats.
  - [Managed Detection & Response (MDR)](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/endpoint-security/managed-detection-response/): 24/7 threat monitoring and response delivered as a managed service.
  - [DNS Protection](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/endpoint-security/dns-protection/): DNS-layer filtering to block malicious domains and web threats.
- [Email Security](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/email-security/): Protect, encrypt, and retain business email.
  - [Email Threat Protection](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/email-security/email-threat-protection/): Filtering against phishing, malware, and spam.
  - [Email Encryption](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/email-security/email-encryption/): Policy-based encryption for sensitive email.
  - [Email Continuity](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/email-security/email-continuity/): Continued email access during outages.
  - [Email Message Privacy](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/email-security/email-message-privacy/): Privacy and data-loss controls for outbound mail.
  - [Security Awareness Training](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/email-security/security-awareness-training/): Phishing simulation and end-user training.
- [Data Backup and Recovery](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/data-backup-recovery/): Automated backup with fast recovery to keep businesses running.
  - [Server Backup](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/data-backup-recovery/server-backup/): Backup and recovery for physical and virtual servers.
  - [Endpoint Backup](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/data-backup-recovery/endpoint-backup/): Backup and recovery for laptops and desktops.
- [SaaS Backup](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/saas-backup/): Backup and recovery for SaaS application data.
  - [Microsoft 365 Backup](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/saas-backup/microsoft-365-backup/): Backup for Exchange, OneDrive, SharePoint, and Teams.
  - [Google Workspace Backup](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/saas-backup/google-workspace-backup/): Backup for Gmail, Drive, and Calendar.
  - [Salesforce Backup](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/saas-backup/salesforce-backup/): Backup and recovery for Salesforce data and metadata.
  - [Box Backup](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/saas-backup/box-backup/): Backup for Box content.
  - [Dropbox Backup](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/saas-backup/dropbox-backup/): Backup for Dropbox content.
- [Continuity and Compliance](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/continuity-compliance/): Minimize downtime, data loss, and compliance risk.
  - [Availability](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/continuity-compliance/availability/): High availability and failover to keep systems running.
  - [Recover](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/continuity-compliance/recover/): Cloud disaster recovery for critical workloads.
  - [Migrate](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/continuity-compliance/migrate/): Workload migration across physical, virtual, and cloud.
  - [Business Communication Archive](https://cybersecurity.opentext.com/products/continuity-compliance/business-communication-archive/): Archiving of email and messaging for compliance and e-discovery.
